Our Mission

Unemployment is a problem. Skills are the solution.

In Nigeria and across Africa, millions of talented youths struggle to find employment, not because they lack potential, but because they lack the right digital skills to compete in today's economy.

CIYA was built to change that. We use AI to make world-class digital skills accessible, affordable, and practical. Whether you want to save money by doing things yourself or earn money by offering services, CIYA gives you everything you need.

Questions

Before you join

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You learn to direct AI tools with clear instructions. Smart tools handle the technical work while you focus on creating and delivering results.

What does it cost?

Beginner courses are 100% free. Advanced is ₦15,000 and Masterclass is ₦30,000, each as a 5-day course.

Which tracks can I join?

Landing Page Creation, E-commerce Website Creation and Portfolio Website Creation. Each one has Beginner, Advanced and Masterclass levels.

How do I earn from what I learn?

Beginner skills help you launch your own site and save money. Advanced and Masterclass teach you to find, price and deliver client projects, so you can offer websites as a service.

When does the next cohort start?

Cohort 4 is coming. Join the WhatsApp community to get launch updates as soon as dates are announced.
